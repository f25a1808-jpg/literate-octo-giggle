🌱 The Bioscience Problem

In Malaysian agro-product chains, minimizing the Feed Conversion Ratio (FCR) is vital for profitability. Broiler chickens do not grow linearly; they follow a sigmoidal (S-shaped) logistic curve.

By identifying the exact point where the growth rate — the derivative f'(t) — peaks and begins to decelerate, farm managers and meat processors can determine the optimal harvest window before feed consumption outpaces muscle deposition.

Data Source Reference: Baseline parameters adapted from the Department of Veterinary Services (DVS) Malaysia Broiler Industry Performance Guidelines (Cobb 500 Breed Standard).

📊 The Calculus Toolkit

Growth Function f(t)
f(t) = L / (1 + a * e^(-b * t))

Predicts total biomass (grams) at day t.

Derivative f'(t) (Rate of Change)
f'(t) = (L * a * b * e^(-b * t)) / (1 + a * e^(-b * t))^2

Calculates exact growth speed (grams/day) at day t.

L (Asymptotic Max Weight): 2900g
a (Initial Scaling Constant): 63.44
b (Growth Coefficient): 0.15
